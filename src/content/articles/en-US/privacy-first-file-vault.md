# What is a privacy-first file vault?

Mo Layer is a private file vault for iPhone. It is designed for photos, videos, screenshots, IDs, contracts, receipts, links, and files that should not sit in the same place as everyday media.

## Why not just use a hidden album?

A hidden album is useful for light privacy. A dedicated vault is better when you need organization, discreet access, file categories, recovery planning, and a clearer privacy boundary.

## What Mo Layer protects

- Private photos and videos
- Sensitive screenshots
- IDs, contracts, receipts, and documents
- Files you want to find quickly without mixing them into your public camera roll

## How privacy works

Mo Layer is local-first. Your content stays on your device by default. If encrypted iCloud sync is enabled, synced content is encrypted before upload, and the developer does not hold your decryption key.

## What Pro adds

Pro focuses on capacity, recovery, and control: unlimited imports, batch organization, encrypted iCloud sync, advanced disguise, decoy controls, intrusion records, and advanced recovery.
