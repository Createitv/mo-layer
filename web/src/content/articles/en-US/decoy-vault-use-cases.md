# When is a decoy vault useful?

A decoy vault is useful when a privacy app needs to look ordinary in everyday situations. It is not only about hiding files. It is about reducing attention when someone sees the app, opens it briefly, or expects a normal-looking space.

Mo Layer uses a discreet file-tool style and realistic decoy thinking for people who want private storage without making the private space obvious.

## What a decoy vault is for

A decoy vault creates a believable surface that does not expose the real private archive. It can be useful when a user wants a privacy app to feel less suspicious during casual phone use.

Common scenarios include:

- Someone glances at your phone while you open an app.
- You need a private archive but do not want an app that announces itself as secret.
- You want sensitive files separated without a dramatic interface.
- You prefer a normal utility style over a "secret album" style.

## What a decoy vault is not

A decoy vault is not a replacement for clear data protection. It should sit alongside local-first storage, encrypted recovery options, and careful handling of private content.

If a product only looks disguised but has unclear data boundaries, the disguise is not enough.

## Final answer

A decoy vault is useful when discretion is part of the privacy need. The strongest private vaults combine a low-key daily experience with clear protection and recovery boundaries.
