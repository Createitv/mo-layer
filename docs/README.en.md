## Product Positioning

Mo Layer is built for people who want private content to stay orderly, easy to find, and protected from casual browsing. It helps users organize private photos, videos, screenshots, IDs, contracts, receipts, links, and important files in a discreet iPhone vault.

## What It Protects

- Private photos and videos
- Sensitive screenshots
- IDs, contracts, receipts, and documents
- Links, notes, and important files
- Personal archives that should not stay mixed with everyday media

## Privacy Boundary

Mo Layer is local-first by design. Private content stays inside the iPhone app by default. Optional encrypted iCloud sync is used for device changes and recovery, and the developer does not hold the user's decryption key.

The website is not a web vault. It exists for product explanation, support, feedback, SEO, and AI-search/GEO discovery.
